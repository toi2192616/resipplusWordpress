Hanoi, the capital of Vietnam, has many international schools. Some Japanese families choose an international school because they would like their children to learn English. Below are six international schools in Hanoi with their addresses, contacts and curricula, followed by information on the Japanese School of Hanoi.

1. Hanoi International School

  • Address: 48 Lieu Giai Street, Giang Vo Ward, Hanoi
  • Phone: (+84 24) 3832 7800 (Admissions: (+84 24) 3832 8140)
  • Email: mainoffice@hisvietnam.com, admissions@hisvietnam.com
  • Website: https://www.hisvietnam.com/
  • Ages and curriculum: ages 4–18; International Baccalaureate (IB) World School (PYP, MYP and Diploma Programme)

2. Singapore International School @ Van Phuc

  • Address: 2D Van Phuc Diplomatic Compound, 46 Van Bao, Ngoc Ha Ward, Hanoi
  • Phone: +84 24 7304 2777
  • Email: enquiry@vanphuc.sis.edu.vn
  • Website: https://vanphuc.sis.edu.vn/
  • Ages and curriculum: kindergarten to high school; combines the Cambridge and Singapore curricula

3. United Nations International School of Hanoi (UNIS Hanoi)

  • Address: G9 Ciputra, Phu Thuong, Hanoi
  • Phone: (+84 24) 7300 4500 (Admissions: (+84 24) 7300 4508)
  • Email: info@unishanoi.org, admissions@unishanoi.org
  • Website: https://www.unishanoi.org/
  • Ages and curriculum: ages 3–18; International Baccalaureate (PYP, MYP and Diploma Programme)

4. Japanese International School (JIS)

  • Address: 84A Nguyen Thanh Binh, Ha Dong Ward, Hanoi (second campus, kindergarten: 25 Nguyen Huy Tuong, Thanh Xuan Ward, Hanoi)
  • Phone: 0973 094 088, 0979 860 088
  • Email: truongquoctenhatban@jis.edu.vn, admissions.jis@jis.edu.vn
  • Website: https://jis.edu.vn/
  • Programmes: early years, a Japanese international programme and a Cambridge international programme, among others

This is a different school from the Japanese School of Hanoi listed below.

5. British International School Hanoi (BIS Hanoi)

  • Address: Hoa Lan Road, Vinhomes Riverside, Phuc Loi Ward, Hanoi
  • Phone: +84 24 3946 0435 (Admissions: extension 888); WhatsApp/Zalo: 0888 602 022
  • Email: bishanoi@bishanoi.com
  • Website: https://www.nordangliaeducation.com/bis-hanoi
  • Ages and curriculum: ages 2–18; British and international curricula, including IGCSE and the IB Diploma Programme

6. St. Paul American School Hanoi

  • Address: New Urban - Splendora, Km10+600 Thang Long Highway, Son Dong, Hanoi
  • Phone: +84 24 3399 6464
  • Email: info@stpaulhanoi.com
  • Website: https://stpaulhanoi.com/
  • Grades and curriculum: Grades 1–12; American curriculum with AP courses
